IN THE HIGH COURT OF KARNATAKA AT BENGALURU

Writ Petition No. 12345 of 2024

Between:
ABC Pvt Ltd ... Petitioner
And
State of Karnataka ... Respondent

1. The petitioner challenges the order dated 01.01.2024 passed by the authority.

2. WHEREAS the parties had entered into a service agreement on 15.06.2023, the
agreement contained an arbitration clause referring disputes to arbitration under
the Arbitration and Conciliation Act, 1996, seated at Bengaluru.

3. The jurisdiction clause provided that only the courts at Bengaluru shall have
jurisdiction over any dispute arising out of the agreement.

4. The respondent contended that liquidated damages of Rs. 5,00,000 were payable
under Section 74 of the Indian Contract Act, 1872 for delay in performance.

5. NOW THEREFORE, having heard the parties, this Court holds that the arbitration
clause is valid and the dispute shall be referred to arbitration. The petition is
disposed of accordingly.
