What is urged is that under section 24 of the Evidence plaintiff alone is not competent to prove the accounts pointed out by the learned District Judge, the account on the face of it does not constitute any suspicion and is seen to be regularly kept. The effect of section 24 is that a new entry in an account book by plaintiff is not evidence of any liability.
