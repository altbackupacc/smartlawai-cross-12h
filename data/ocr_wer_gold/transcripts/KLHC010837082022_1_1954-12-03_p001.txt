Judgment.

This appeal arises out of a dispute in respect of the compensation amount awarded in respect of the property involved in the case. Parties 1 and 2 have set up rival claims. The first party claims the property as his common property and contends that the second party has only a known right over the property. This case was accepted by the lower court and the compensation amount has been apportioned on that basis.
