This appeal having been finally heard on 10.11.1954 the court on the same day delivered the following

Judgment.
(Delivered by Subramania Iyer J.).

We are glad to note that the fight between father and son which has led to this appeal has been amicably adjusted by them out of court. Both parties have put in a compromise petition in this court, C.M.P. 1689 of 1954 which is allowed. The appeal is accordingly allowed in terms of the compromise and the suit dismissed without any order for costs either here or in the court below. Refund half the court fee to the appellant's advocate.
