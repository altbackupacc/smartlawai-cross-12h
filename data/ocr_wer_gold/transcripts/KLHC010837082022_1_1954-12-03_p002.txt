It is further seen that as early as in the year 1000 this property was dealt with in Ext.3 partition good executed by the members of the first party. They have dealt with this property as their own. Reference is also made to a Kenan Jerise in favour of Verkittarion Vedhyar who is the predecessor in interest of the second party. Ext.8 has been produced as the Kenan Jerise. It is an ancient dead and has been produced from proper custody. The lower court has held that it is a genuine document. The mere fact that the signature in Ext.3 is somewhat different from the same person's signature in Exts V and VI of later dates, is not a sufficient reason to discard Ext.8 as a spurious document. The weight of the evidence on memory is decidedly in favour of the case set up by the first party and hence we see no reason to disturb the conclusion arrived at by the lower court.

This appeal fails and is dismissed with costs.
