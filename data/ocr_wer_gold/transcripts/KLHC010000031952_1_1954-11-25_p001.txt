This appeal suit having been finally heard on 25-11-1954, this court on the same day delivered the following:

Judgment.

The lower court declined to pass a decree for rent for 18 years charged on the equity of redemption on the ground that even though the lease good provided for a charge for arrears that would apply only to the period of the lease fixed by the deed and not so the case of holding over. The cases followed by the learned Judge in support of this view have all been considered in a recent Full Bench decision of this court in A.S. 101/58 where it was held that such a term really formed part of the lease arrangement and would ensure even for the period of the holding-over. In view of this decision the learned counsel for the respondent had to conclude that the appeal has to be allowed.

Accordingly we modify the lower court's decree by granting a decree.
