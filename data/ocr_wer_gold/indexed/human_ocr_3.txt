This Appeal Suit having been finally heard on 23-8-1957, this court on the same day delivered the following:

JUDGMENT.

In view of the Travancore-Cochin Compensation for Tenants Improvements Act, (X of 1956) this suit has become superfileous. It is up to the parties to work out their rights and remedies in the proceedings and execute the redemption decree in O.S. 362 of 1103 of the Meerachil Munsiff's Court. With this observation this appeal is allowed and the suit giving rise to it is dismissed, with the direction that the parties shall bear their costs throughout.

Order accordingly.
