This Appeal Suit having been finally heard on 8-18-1956, this court on the same day delivered the following:-

JUDGMENT.

Of the three heads under which enhancement is claimed we are inclined to think that the value awarded for the bigger building is not adequate. It was a new construction and according to the appellant it was fetching a rent of Rs. 10/- per month. Evidence let in by the State shows that the value was fixed by estimating the cost of building materials at 150% above the schedule rates. It is common knowledge that in 1185 the usual rate was 200 to 300% above the schedule rates. We accordingly hold that the value of that trini building should be fixed on the basis that on the date of the relevant notification the value of building materials was at least 200% above the scheduled rates. On that footing the appellant is entitled to get Rs. 130-10-8 more for the bigger building and we award that amount also to him together with the usual solution of 15% thereon and interest on the aggregate of the two at 6% per annum from the date of dispossession.

The claim for enhancement in respect of other items, namely, the smaller building, the wells and trees is unsustainable.
