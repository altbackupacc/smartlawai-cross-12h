This appeal suit having been heard on 6-6-1952, the court on the same day delivered the following:

Judgment

The plea of limitation is against the provisions of Act 180 of the Limitation Act (Travancore) and since the suit was filed within 12 years of the date of demand mentioned in that article it is not barred by limitation. The partial discharge pleaded by the defendant is not satisfactorily made out. The only remaining question relates to the mode of calculation. The amount as due on 21-3-1107 has to be calculated and the sum due to the defendant under the B class ticket is to be deducted. It was admitted on both sides, that such deduction, the amount due will be Indian K.666-9-0. This along with the moiety of the principal will be the amount due to the plaintiff on the date of suit. Thereafter interest at 4 per cent on the principal sum of K.666-9-0 will be calculated till 28-11-1125 when K.500 had been deposited by the defendant in court. This will be deducted on that day; and 4 per cent interest will be calculated on the balance till the date of decree of the lower court. The amount found due then together with interest thereon at 4 per cent, and one-half of the costs of the plaintiff in the lower court will be the sum due to the plaintiff.
The amount due to the plaintiff on the date of suit will be the amount due to the plaintiff on the date of suit. Thereafter interest at 4 per cent on the principal sum of K.666-9-0 will be calculated till 28-11-1125 when K.500 had been deposited by the defendant in court. This will be deducted on that day; and 4 per cent interest will be calculated on the balance till the date of decree of the lower court. The amount found due then together with interest thereon at 4 per cent, and one-half of the costs of the plaintiff in the lower court will be the sum due to the plaintiff.
