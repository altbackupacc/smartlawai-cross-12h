This appeal suit having been heard on the 7th February 1950, this court on the same item delivered the following:

Judgment.

The appeal is against an order settling a proclamation. It would appear there is controversy between the parties as to whether one of the encumbrances reserved via., the decree dett one under 0.3.294 of 1097 is a live one. The lower court will direct the proclamation to be suitably amended so as to incorporate into it that the parties are not agreed as to the subsistence of the debt. As for the other objections so long as the lower court has not gone into the correctness of the amounts reserved for the decree 0.3. No.105 of 1105 and the amounts due per the decree under execution there is no order which would preclude the parties from agitating at the appropriate time what the correct amounts due are with these observations the appeal is dismissed, but the proclamation will be suitably amended with reference to the first point mentioned above.

Order accordingly.

7th February 1950.
