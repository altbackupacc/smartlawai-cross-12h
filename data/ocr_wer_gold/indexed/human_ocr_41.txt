This is an appeal by the plaintiff in 0.S.No. 30 of 1124 of the District Court of Mavelikara. He died subsequent to the filing of the appeal and his heirs have been implicated as additional appellants, 2 to 4, in pursuance of the order in C.M.P. 1509 of 1955 dated the 14th August 1956.

2. The present suit, 0.S.No. 30 of 1124, is a suit to set aside the ex parte decree in 0.S.No. 45 of 1112 of the District Court of Quilon. The plaintiff had filed a petition to set aside the ex parte decree on the ground of fraud in the service of summons. That petition, it seems, was dismissed by the trial court and the dismissal confirmed by the High Court
