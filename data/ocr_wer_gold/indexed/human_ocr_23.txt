This annual prisoner of a suit for recovery of accounts due per account, the defendant being the applicant. The suit was filed by the plaintiff who was the proprietor of a hotel called B. V. Brohm's Hotel at Fortran claiming that as per dealings between himself and the defendant as evidenced by the accounts kept by him, 2.2 was due to him. The defendant while admitting dealings with the plaintiff denied his identity for any amount. The lower court opened the plaintiff's claim.

B. xa.1 to D are the relevant pages in the Lodger kept by the plaintiff denoting the transactions with the defendant. Ex. 9.40 of 1181, Ex. 6 of 1128, Ex. 6 of 1193 and Ex. 1 of 1194. Ex. 7 shows that the B. V. Hotel was purchased by the plaintiff only in 1180. Ex. 8 to G shows the Day book entries of the relevant transactions. The amounts disclosed on the basis of these accounts consist of various kinds of claims. Besides being the proprietor of the B. V. Hotel the plaintiff was a partner in a total called Ananda Mandiram and a provision store called Velour Store. According to the plaintiff, the defendant had transactions with all these three concerns and he had agreed to the amount due as per his dealings with Velour Store and Ananda Mandiram Hotel to be brought into the B. V. accounts, thus there being a consolidated account showing the state of dealings with the defendant. Do the accounts have claimed are items composed of debt due as a result of direct transactions with the B. V. Hotel and those due as per dealings with the other two concerns.
