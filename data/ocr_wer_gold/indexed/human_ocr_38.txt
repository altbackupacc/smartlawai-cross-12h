This Appeal having been heard on 19-11-1958, the court on the same day delivered the following:

JUDICIAL

This is an appeal by the defendants 11 and 12, the legal representatives of the deceased second defendant, against the decree of the lower court negativing their rights to get the share of the second defendant in the suit properties.

It is not necessary for us to go in any very great detail as we have only to find out whether the second defendant has a separate share as was entitled to a share in his individual right, which could be passed on to his legal representatives, viz., the appellants.

During the arguments of this case before us, it has been brought to our notice that the second defendant died long before the filing of the written statement by the 1st defendant.

If that is so, the rights of the 2nd defendants in the properties had already lapsed to the 1st defendant and there was nothing that the appellants could get as representatives of the second defendant, because he was not entitled to any separate share as such from the property. The second defendant died on 28th Medan 1115 and the written statement of 1st defendant has been filed on B-12-1114.

In this view it is unnecessary for us to consider the argument of Mr. Potti, as to whether the 1st defendant had delivered his intention to separate in his written statement filed.

This appeal fails and is dismissed; but without any order as to costs.

18-11-58
